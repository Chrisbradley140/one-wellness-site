Kingdom Centre, Al Faisaliah, and a lot of people working hours that make a gym membership decorative.

The district

Al Olaya is central Riyadh’s business district — towers, hotels, offices and high-rise residential, with the Kingdom Centre and Al Faisaliah Tower on its skyline. Residents here are more likely to live in an apartment than a villa, and more likely to be time-poor than space-rich.

Training in your space

Apartment training is its own discipline. Downstairs facilities are often better than people assume, and where they are not, a well-chosen kit list and a floor plan will do more than a membership. Noise and neighbours are part of the design, not an afterthought.

Early mornings and late evenings are normal here. Sessions are booked around your working day, not the other way round.
